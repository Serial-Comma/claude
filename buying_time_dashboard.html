Buying Time in Singapore

Money buys time, time buys advantage, accumulated advantage constitutes and reproduces class. The paradigm case is the family car — made exclusionary by the COE — measured here against the one commute every secondary-school household makes twice a day.

Traffic correction, measured against the real AM window

OneMap's drive routing has no congestion model — only free-flow times. The placeholder ×1.35 AM multiplier has been replaced with a factor measured directly against the target window.

Primary correction (applied to all AM car times above): Google Maps' “Arrive by 7:30 AM, Fri 18 Sep 2026” predictive-traffic duration was queried for 10 routes spanning all four distance bands, and compared against OneMap's free-flow time for the same routes. Pooled ratio: ×1.175 (n=10) — lower than the ×1.35 placeholder it replaces, and non-monotonic with distance (short arterial-heavy trips ran relatively more congested than long expressway-heavy ones). This is genuine predictive traffic for the exact target arrival time, not a proxy.
Secondary check: a 20-route live-TomTom sample (5/band, Thu 2026-09-17 ~18:45 SGT — an evening snapshot, not the AM window) showed delay scaling with distance instead, from ~0% under 5 km to 18–26% beyond 10 km. The two sources disagree on which distance band is most congested at peak; the Google figure is used because it targets the actual 07:00–07:30 window the study measures, while TomTom's historical-traffic mode returned zero predicted delay for future AM departures on this account.

Methodology & limitations

What changed from the modelled exhibit

  • Analytic distance-decay estimates replaced with real OneMap drive/pt routing (191 successful routes across two samples).
  • HDB estates rebuilt from the authoritative HDB Property Information register (13,357 blocks, dwelling-unit–weighted k-means), fixing the block-count undercount in Bedok and Ang Mo Kio.
  • School list replaced with the official MOE General Information of Schools directory, filtered to the 133 pure-secondary institutions and geocoded via OneMap Search.
  • Peak-hour AM car correction replaced with a measured ×1.175 factor (Google Maps “arrive by 07:30” predictive traffic vs. OneMap free-flow, n=10 routes, all distance bands); PM leg keeps the ×1.15 placeholder, unmeasured.

Known limitations carried forward

  • Random pairing over-samples distance (mean 11.4 km) relative to real enrolment; the <5 km band is the conservative floor.
  • 7 (Sample A) and 2 (Sample B) pairs failed to route (OneMap PT itinerary not found) and are excluded, not imputed.
  • PT arrival-by-07:30 solved by iterative depart-at queries (2–3 iterations to converge within 2 min); AM peak car time is free-flow × a placeholder multiplier, not true AM-window traffic.
  • HDB block→coordinate join covers 97.3% of blocks (newest Tengah/Bishan/Hougang BTOs fall outside the ~2017–2021 vintage address dump).
